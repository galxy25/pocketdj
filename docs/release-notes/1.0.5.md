# PocketDJ 1.0.5 — App Store release notes

DRAFT 2026-11-07, branch feat/remote-shareplay; DJ Mode added 2026-11-09 (feat/dj-mode, Levi: "all as part of 1.0.5"). The headlines are DJ Mode (iOS/iPadOS/macOS/visionOS; not tvOS) and Remote, which lives in the Jukebox panel (Jukebox Hero | Remote switch, merged 2026-11-08): SharePlay
listening, Backseat, and Mix Together on two decks. macOS only (folded in 2026-11-10 after iOS/tvOS/visionOS had already released): derived pocket runs (merge cfba3db6). The release also carries the vinyl work
(merge 67dffcc2): each album side plays as one file, and the album page has a waveform scrubber
and editor. The 4-deck jam is a separate later release, so these notes don't mention it.

Rights: a device in a session only plays what it can play itself, from its own library or the
listener's own Apple Music. Rips are never sent to other people's devices.

Plain text only: App Store Connect rejects "♥" in whatsNew.

## iOS / iPadOS / visionOS

```text
NEW: DJ MODE
Tap DJ next to Mix Mode in Now Playing and PocketDJ mixes your queue like a DJ:
• Songs blend into each other with the same transitions as the Mix tab: crossfades, effect sweeps, and tempo and key matching.
• The set builds the tempo up in waves and brings it back down, keeping songs of a similar genre, era and collection together.
• Volume leveling keeps every song at the same loudness.
• Skip mixes into the next song too. Double-tap Skip for a fast sweep.
• Watch the mix happen: the tempo, pitch, effects and volume controls in Now Playing move as each song blends into the next.
• Whatever you choose to play next always plays next. PocketDJ arranges the rest around it.
Turn each part on or off, and set the crossfade and skip lengths, in the new Settings > Mixing page.

MIX
• New Volume Glide option evens out the volume between songs in Auto mode. Turn it on in Settings > Mixing.

NEW: REMOTE
Jukebox now has a Remote side to listen and mix with friends over SharePlay:
• Listen Together: share your queue over FaceTime or with a nearby iPhone. Everyone hears the same song at the same moment, and anyone can skip, add or reorder.
• Backseat: plug a friend's phone into the car and run the music from yours. Your phone stays silent and becomes the remote.
• Mix Together: two people, two decks. Each of you runs a deck, you share the crossfader, and every device plays the mix live.
Each device plays from its own library or Apple Music.

VINYL
• Playing an album now plays the whole side as one file, so songs no longer cut off early.
• The album page shows the waveform of the whole album. Tap or drag to jump anywhere.
• Drag the song markers to fix where a song starts or ends, and double-tap a song to rename it.
```
