## iOS / iPadOS

```text
NEW: MIX MODE IN NOW PLAYING
Turn on Mix Mode from Now Playing and PocketDJ loads the current and next few songs onto your device as they play, so stems, effects, tempo and the rest of the mini-mix controls work in any playlist, pocket or queue, not just on songs you've already downloaded.
• Songs loaded this way clean themselves up after 48 hours, unless you keep them, add them to a mix or download them
• Settings ▸ Storage ▸ Mix Mode: how long to keep songs, a size limit, how far ahead to load, cellular use, Keep all and Clear now
• Optional: start every new session in Mix Mode

NEW: FX LOCK
Lock your effects (compressor, reverb, flanger, filter) and they stay on for every next song, fading in smoothly at each change. Stem Lock gets smarter too: with vocals muted, a song still plays as an instrumental even if only a different edition of it is on your device.

NEW: POCKET WHEEL
History ▸ Pockets now shows a color wheel of what you listen to by hour and day of the week. Tap the center to play the pocket that fits right now: a 100-song run that adjusts as you skip. Also in CarPlay (For You) and Siri ("Play my pocket in PocketDJ"). Optionally let it notice walking and running to tune the picks.

COLLECTIONS
• Add to Playlist on every song and album menu: your top suggestions with checkmarks, plus More… to search
• Remove duplicates from a pocket or playlist, or from all your collections at once

PLAYBACK & CARPLAY
• CarPlay no longer shows an old song or resumes the wrong one after an Apple Music track ends
• Smoother, quieter hand-offs between Apple Music and downloaded songs, even on a locked phone
• Downloaded songs no longer skip when playing over AirPlay
• Fewer stalls when the next song is still loading on a weak signal
• Pressing play on the Lock Screen or in the car resumes your paused session

MIX
• Auto Mix no longer gets stuck while a crate is still downloading, and its progress is accurate
• The crate picker no longer jumps back to the top while a crate downloads
• Songs no longer cut off early while they're still arriving

ALSO
• Fixed a freeze at launch on very large libraries
• Apple Music playlist sync recovers on its own when Apple's sign-in needs a refresh
```
