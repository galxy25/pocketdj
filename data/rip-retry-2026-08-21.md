## What fixed the other 33
The 4-day "wedged Music.app" stall was a MISDIAGNOSIS by the rip skill's own error text. Duplicate library rows: beside each real track sits a placeholder with an empty `kind` and no playable asset. Music ACCEPTS `play t` on it, answers `duration` with a real number, and silently does nothing — so the health probe read leftover state and blamed the playback engine.
- `b5c7db49` — pick a playable row in both play paths; fall back to a playable duplicate; verify after play that the player actually landed on the requested track (`wrong-track`), so a no-op play can never record the WRONG song under this song's name.
- `c95faf9f` — rows with `cloud status = no longer available` behave identically; excluded too. Several songs kept a playable uploaded copy beside the dead subscription row.

To resume later (if these are re-acquired): the driver reads `~/.pocketdj/backfill/rip-backfill-state.json`; clear the ids from `failed` **with the driver STOPPED** (it overwrites the file from memory while running), then `launchctl bootstrap`.
