Your music. Two decks. One tap.

A real DJ rig, built from the music you already own.

Start with your Apple Music, or plug in your own sources like vinyl you've digitized. Nothing is preloaded, and nothing leaves your device unless you ask.

Mix

Two decks. Blend for real.

Put a track on each deck and ride the crossfader. Every track shows its BPM and Camelot key, so you can spot a harmonic match before you commit to it.

  • Tempo, pitch and 3-band EQ on every deck
  • Effects rack: Punch, Hall, Flanger and a low-pass sweep
  • Cue points and loops to jump straight to the drop
  • Stems: pull the vocal out mid-phrase
  • Auto-DJ runs the night with timed crossfades and key-aware glides. Grab the wheel whenever you like
  • Record the whole set while it plays
  • Streaming mode starts a deck while the song is still arrivingComing in 1.0.2
  • Crate A/B auto-mixes between two collectionsComing in 1.0.2
Producer

A studio next to your library.

Not a separate app to export into. Pull a sound out of any track and build with it right there.

  • Samples and loops sliced from your own songs
  • 16-step sequencer with per-row mixer decks
  • Software instruments to play live
  • Demuxer: split a song into stems and pull out its chords and lyrics
  • Tracks: arrange it all on a multitrack timeline with beat matching
Now Playing

One deck that follows you.

A gold record that spins at the song's tempo, with your queue, transport and up-next exactly where you left them, on whichever screen you pick up next.

  • CarPlay and Lock Screen controls
  • Home Screen widgets for what's playing
  • Siri and Shortcuts to start a playlist hands-free
  • Stems in Now PlayingComing in 1.0.2
Browse

Every song, read like a DJ.

Your whole library by album, song or artist, each with tempo, Camelot key and length at a glance. Search by artist, album or genre, and filter down to exactly the crate you need.

  • Recognize a song playing nearby and add it to your library on the spot
Organize

Playlists, Pockets and Setlists.

Playlists for the quick stuff. Pockets are reusable blocks you build sets out of. Setlists run start to finish, or hand any of them to Auto-DJ.

In the Pocket.

PocketDJ bets on three or four songs you'll play straight through right now, based on the time of day and, if you turn on places & trips, where you are. Play them without skipping to land the pocket, then type or dictate how it felt. Every round teaches it your taste.

  • Listening Pockets finds the runs you play without skipping and what they share: artists, genres, tempo, time of day. Off until you turn it on.

Jukebox

Host a request line at a party. Guests scan a code and queue songs from your library, no app to download.

Games

Gem Collector and Music with Friends turn your library into play, with In the Pocket joining the lineup.

Your sources
