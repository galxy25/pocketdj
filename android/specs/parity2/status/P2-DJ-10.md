## Summary
With DJ Mode on, the next 40 upcoming rows are re-ordered by DjSetPlanner into tempo waves and pockets. The listener's Play Next pick (djUserNext at index+1) is fixed in place, never preceded, spent when it plays, and re-anchors the wave (also when the sequencer cleared the hold before the track-start re-plan). Fixed rows: pick, album-run blocks, rip / lane fetch / buffer stream in flight, rows deferred >= 12, and the next row when local/buffering/mid-transition/unknown length/< 30 s runway. One uid-verified `applyDjOrder` per re-plan; duplicate uids bail. Pocket-run rows (appendRunRows) are unpinned planner material and artist spacing 4 holds on them (tested). Shuffle's edit is re-planned over, so the planner order supersedes it. Log (`adb logcat -s PocketDJ.dj`): `replan reason=<track|edit|on|membership|analysis> song=.. rows=40 moved=N fixed=K pick=<uid|-> band=.. dir=.. next=..`.
